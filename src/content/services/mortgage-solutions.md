Financing for ready and off-plan residential properties such as plots, premium villas, townhouses, apartments and multiple units across UAE. We support resident and non-resident borrowers and both Salaried and Self-Employed profiles, matching your case to the most suitable lender and policy fit.

Low-Doc Mortgages are designed for clients who have strong income and assets but limited income documentation. Low-doc mortgages are typically geared towards Self-Employed Individuals, Freelancers and Building Contractors with variable or irregular income.
